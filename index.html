What makes Waddat Beverage special?
Waddat Beverage is made following authentic Jamaican recipes, using the finest natural ingredients. No artificial flavors, no compromises—just pure goodness in every sip!
Is there caffeine in Waddat drinks?
Some of our traditional drinks contain natural ingredients that may provide a mild, natural energy boost without the jitters!
How much sugar is in Waddat drinks?
We carefully balance our recipes to maintain traditional flavors while keeping sugar levels in check. The final product has just a touch of natural sweetness.
Can kids drink Waddat?
Absolutely! Our natural drinks are a great alternative to sugary sodas. However, for some of our traditional recipes, we recommend consulting your pediatrician for younger children.
How should I store Waddat drinks?
Keep them chilled! Refrigeration helps maintain their freshness and authentic flavor profile over time.
Can I drink Waddat every day?
Yes! Many of our fans enjoy a bottle daily as part of their healthy routine. Listen to your body and enjoy as much as you like!
