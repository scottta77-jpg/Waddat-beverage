OUR ROOTS

EVERY BOTTLE HAS A
BEGINNING.

Before there was a bottle, a label or a name, there was a little girl on her grandad’s farm in Jamaica. Surrounded by fresh fruit and familiar flavours, she discovered that the best things were made with care and shared with others.

Illustrative scene
1985

A FARM. A GRANDAD.
A FIRST BATCH.

On her grandad’s farm, our founder helped prepare the first homemade batch of what would one day inspire Waddat. Sorrel, ginger and tropical fruit brought everyone to the table. For her, those moments became the taste of home.

1995

THE RECIPES TRAVELLED.
THE ROOTS STAYED.

A decade later, a new chapter in the UK brought new surroundings, but the flavours of Jamaica came too. Family gatherings became a place to share those much-loved recipes, keeping a connection to home alive, one glass at a time.

Illustrative scene
2025

A FAMILY INSPIRATION.
A BRAND OF ITS OWN.

In 2025, Waddat officially launched, turning years of inspiration into a range of Jamaican-inspired drinks. From bold sorrel and warming ginger to tropical sea moss blends, the aim was simple: bring island flavour to more people, in a bottle ready to share.

THE NEXT CHAPTER

OUR ROOTS RUN DEEP.
OUR STORY KEEPS GROWING.

From the farm to your local shop, the spirit stays the same: bold flavour, warm memories and something good to share. Whether you grew up with these tastes or are discovering them for the first time, there’s a place for you in the Waddat story.
